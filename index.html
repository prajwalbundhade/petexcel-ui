Meet the Pet Excel
Marketing Team

Co-founder

Varnit Dhoundiyal

Varnit Dhoundiyal, Co-founder and Pet Industry Marketing Expert, began his journey in the pet industry while working with a generalist marketing agency based in Florida, USA.

The agency served a diverse portfolio of clients, ranging from clothing brands and manufacturing companies to private jet and travel businesses.

Fortunately, Varnit got the opportunity to manage the agency's pet and animal health industry portfolio.

It was during this experience that he discovered his passion for marketing, combined with his love for animals.

After gaining the necessary industry experience and expertise, Varnit decided to start his own agency, Pet Excel Marketing, dedicated exclusively to the pet industry.

Today, Pet Excel Marketing has worked with 350+ pet brands globally, helping businesses across the pet and animal health industry strengthen their marketing, accelerate growth, and make a lasting impact.

Jahanvi Bhalla, Co-founder of Pet Excel Marketing, has loved animals her whole life. That passion inspired her to co-found Pet Excel Marketing, a marketing agency dedicated exclusively to the pet industry.

She believes pet brands deserve the opportunity to reach a global audience and compete on an international stage.

For Jahanvi, marketing goes beyond campaigns and client accounts. She believes in building genuine relationships, understanding each brand's vision, and working alongside founders as an extension of their teams.

Her approach is built on collaboration and genuine passion, treating every brand's growth and success as her own.

Challenges Pet Brands Face Today

↗

Rising Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC)

Increasing advertising costs make it harder for pet brands to acquire new customers profitably.

▣

Market Saturation & Competition

Growing competition in pet food, supplements, treats, and accessories makes standing out difficult.

♢

Building Consumer Trust

Pet parents demand transparency, product quality, safety, and credible proof before purchasing.

♡

Customer Retention & Loyalty

Turning first-time pet buyers into repeat customers and long-term brand advocates remains a challenge.

▦

Brand Differentiation & Positioning

Pet brands struggle to communicate what makes their products unique in an overcrowded market.
